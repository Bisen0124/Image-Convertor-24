Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Key Privacy Principle: Fast Image Convertor operates entirely on your device. We do not upload, collect, or store your photos on any server.

1. 100% Client-Side In-Browser Processing

Unlike standard web converters that upload your pictures to remote cloud servers, Fast Image Convertor executes image conversions locally in your web browser using HTML5 Canvas and WebAssembly technologies. Your original files, converted outputs, and personal images never leave your local hardware.

2. No Account or Registration Required

You can use Fast Image Convertor freely without creating an account, entering an email address, or logging in. We do not maintain any user database or account profiles.

3. Analytics & Usage Data

We use Google Analytics (GA4) to evaluate general aggregated site performance, such as page views and browser types, to improve site speed and reliability. Google Analytics collects anonymized interaction data without tracking personal identity.

4. Google AdSense & Cookies

We use third-party advertising companies, including Google AdSense, to serve advertisements when you visit our website. Google uses cookies, including the DoubleClick cookie, to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites on the Internet.

  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • You may also opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

5. Contact Information

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, feel free to visit our homepage at fastimageconvertor.com.
